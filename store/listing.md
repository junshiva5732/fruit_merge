### Full description (4,000)
```
🍉 Fruit Merge – Drop. Merge. Watermelon!

Drop a fruit into the box. When two of the same fruit touch, they merge into the next one —
cherry → strawberry → grape → tangerine → persimmon → apple → pear → peach → pineapple → melon → WATERMELON!
Just don't let the pile cross the red line.
No sign-up, no internet required — open it and play.

✨ Features

• Satisfying physics
  Fruits roll, bounce and stack realistically. Every merge gives the pile a little bump — plan your chain reactions!

• 60 stages on a world map
  Travel through 6 regions — Apple Orchard, Strawberry Field, Tropical Beach, Sunset Hills, Starlight Garden and Rainbow Castle.
  Reach target scores, build specific fruits, or break stones on the floor with a limited number of fruits. Collect up to 3 stars per stage.

• Endless mode
  How high can you score? The challenge rises as you go, with auto-drop timers in the later game.

• Special fruits
  🌈 Rainbow fruit upgrades whatever it touches. 💣 Bombs blast the pile apart. 🪨 Stones won't merge — crack them with combos or the hammer.

• Combos
  Merge again within one second for ×2, ×3 … up to ×5 points!

• Hammer & continue
  Stuck? Smash a fruit with the hammer, or continue once per game after a short ad.

• Challenge your friends
  Share a score card to any app. Friends who open the link play to beat your score!

🎮 Perfect for
• Quick breaks on the bus or subway
• Fans of merge games and relaxing puzzles
• Anyone who loves the "just one more drop" feeling

📱 Light and comfortable
Portrait mode, one-handed play, cheerful music. Music, sound effects and vibration can each be turned off.
Available in English, Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese — switch languages in the app.

🔒 Privacy-friendly
Your scores and progress stay on your phone and are never uploaded to any server.

---
※ This app contains ads.
```
